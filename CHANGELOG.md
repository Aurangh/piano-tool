# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-19
- Chord engine with correct theoretical spelling: triads, sus, 6ths and 7ths (C°7 = C E♭ G♭ B𝄫), plus all inversions.
- New **Chords** tab (Explorer): pick a root and quality to see the chord on the staff and keyboard, its degrees, intervals, inversion, figured bass and alternative symbols; play it as a block or arpeggio; cycle keys by 4ths or 5ths; "Try it on the keys" checks your chord and inversion.
- Play view names what you play (chord symbol, inversion, alternative names; intervals for two notes) and respells the notes to fit the chord.
- Settings: chord symbol style — Standard (Cmaj7, Cm7), Jazz (CΔ7, C−7, Cø7), Classical (CM7, Cmin7).
- Staff: chord accidentals are staggered so they no longer overlap.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-19
- Standalone app: installable on iPad, iPhone, Android and Linux, with an offline cache and an update banner.
- Storage moved to IndexedDB, with localStorage kept as a fallback.
- Backup files (.json) can be saved and opened; songs can be imported and exported as .txt files.
- Computer-keyboard playing and shortcuts.
- Settings: version, install help and update check.

## 0.2.0
- Full view, with only the staff and keyboard showing.
- Reading-only mode with the keyboard hidden.
- Song library: 9 songs, chord symbols, and Listen / Wait for me / Step modes.

## 0.1.0
- Touch keyboard, synth sound, grand staff.
- Note-reading trainer (6 levels), key-signature quiz, circle of fifths, progress screen.
